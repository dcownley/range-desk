Bluetooth Explorer

Records the raw data a Bluetooth instrument sends, so its format can be worked out. Use it for the Calypso Weatherdot, or for a Calypso wind meter that Range Desk can't read. It only reads; it changes nothing on the instrument.

1. Connect

  1. Close Anemotracker and any other app using the instrument, so it is free.
  2. Tap Connect and pick the instrument from the list.

Not connected.

2. Note what the app or screen shows

Every minute or so, and whenever the reading changes (breathe on it, hold it in your hand, move it into the sun), type what Anemotracker shows and tap Note. The notes are what make the decoding possible.

3. Send it to Claude

After 5 to 10 minutes with a few notes, save the file and send it in the chat.
