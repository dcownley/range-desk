Predicted against observed, per logged string

Observed = what you dialled, corrected by where the group centre landed. Elevation: + means the solver asked for too little, − too much. Windage: + means the group needed more right than predicted. A consistent elevation difference across distances points to muzzle velocity; one that grows with distance points to BC.
